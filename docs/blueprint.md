# **App Name**: Salon Elegance

## Core Features:

- Service Catalog: Display available nail and beauty services with descriptions and prices.
- Appointment Booking: Allow customers to book appointments online, specifying service, date, and time.
- Image Gallery: Showcase high-quality photos of nail art and salon interior.
- Location and Contact: Display the salon's address (173 Bram Fischer, Randburg), contact information, and a map.
- Customer Testimonials: Display customer reviews and testimonials to build trust and credibility.
- AI Style Recommendation Tool: Utilize an AI to analyze user-uploaded images and provide personalized style recommendations, suggesting nail designs or beauty treatments based on user preferences. The LLM acts as a 'tool' by applying reasoning to assess user input, compare it to salon's service offerings, and incorporate relevant details (like current trends or salon specialties) in its suggestions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep violet (#79579E), conveying sophistication and creativity. Chosen for its association with beauty and relaxation.
- Background color: Light lavender (#EEEAFD), offering a calming and clean backdrop that enhances readability.
- Accent color: Soft rose (#C499BE), providing a gentle contrast that complements the primary and background colors.
- Headline font: 'Belleza' sans-serif, used for an artful look with beauty, fashion, and design.
- Body font: 'Alegreya' serif, suitable for elegant, intellectual, contemporary feel.
- Use elegant, custom icons related to beauty services and salon features.
- Subtle, smooth transitions and animations to create a luxurious browsing experience.
